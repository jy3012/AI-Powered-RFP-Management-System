# AI RFP System - Quick Explanation

## 🎯 What It Does (30 seconds)

**Automated RFP management system** that uses AI to:
- Generate RFPs from plain English
- Send emails to vendors automatically  
- Read vendor replies and extract data
- Compare proposals and recommend the best one

**Result:** 90% time savings, zero manual data entry

---

## 🔄 How It Works (Simple Flow)

```
1. YOU WRITE: "Need 50 laptops, $50k budget"
   ↓
2. AI CREATES: Structured RFP automatically
   ↓
3. SYSTEM SENDS: Emails to vendors
   ↓
4. VENDOR REPLIES: "We can do $48k, 25 days"
   ↓
5. AI READS: Extracts price, delivery, terms
   ↓
6. AI COMPARES: Scores all proposals
   ↓
7. YOU DECIDE: See ranked recommendations
```

**Time: 10 minutes vs 5 hours manually** ⚡
